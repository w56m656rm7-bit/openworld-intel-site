WHO WE ARE

About OpenWorld Intel

Independent by design

OpenWorld Intel is an independent editorial project for open-world gaming fans. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

We distinguish official statements, editorial analysis and information that has not yet been verified.

Privacy & transparency

This starter site uses no advertising trackers or newsletter forms. Your progress checklist is stored only in your own browser using local storage; we don't receive it.

Before adding analytics, adverts, affiliates or subscriptions, we'll add appropriate privacy notices, disclosures and consent controls. A public contact address will also be added before commercial launch.

We don't publish fabricated missions, collectibles, interactive maps or gameplay findings. Content and features will expand only when we can verify the underlying information.
